Now you can view, export, and import your Chrome history in a couple of clicks. Everything stays on your device.

Don't see the icon? Click in the toolbar and pin Quick Chrome History.

Keep your history past 90 days

Chrome deletes your browsing history after about 90 days. Open the extension and turn on Auto-export to save a fresh copy every day or week.
