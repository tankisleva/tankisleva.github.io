Now you can save your Chrome history as a CSV, Excel, or JSON file in a couple of clicks. Everything stays on your device.

Click the Quick Chrome History Export icon in your toolbar to export your history.

google.com

Don't see the icon? Click in the toolbar and pin Quick Chrome History Export.

Keep your history past 90 days

Chrome deletes browsing history after about 90 days. Open the extension and turn on Auto-export to save a fresh copy every day or week.
